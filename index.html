Market Research

Market Research is a personal Chrome extension made by Ashwin Kumar. It helps with product research: it collects public discussions and reviews about a topic, saves them to the user's own database, writes an AI summary of the main pain points, and shows how often people search Google for those pain points.

How it uses Google data

With the user's permission (Google sign-in), it uses the Google Ads API's Keyword Planner to read average monthly searches, competition and bid estimates for search phrases. It does not create, change or pay for ads, and it does not read campaigns, billing or any other account data.
