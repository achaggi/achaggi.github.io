Privacy policy

This policy covers the Market Research Chrome extension, published by Ashwin Kumar.

What it accesses

When you connect your Google account, the extension asks for the Google Ads scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords). It uses it only to call Keyword Planner and to list the Google Ads accounts you can use, so it can show search volumes, competition and bid estimates for search phrases you research.

Where data is kept

Your sign-in is kept only in your own browser. Keyword results are saved only to the database you connect in the extension's settings. Nothing is sent to the publisher or to anyone else, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.

Google user data

The extension's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Click Disconnect in the extension, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it kept in your browser.
